What they are

Compete for real. Organised the right way.

Our tournaments are not a one-off match or an improvised event. They are competitions designed from start to finish to be played at a high level from the first minute: quality matches, refereeing, well-distributed playing time, breaks and an experience that does not end at the final whistle. This is not about turning up for the sake of it: it is about competing, learning and enjoying on equal terms.
